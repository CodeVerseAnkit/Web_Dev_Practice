Maggi Recipe

Ingredients :-

  • 1 packet Maggi noodles
  • 1½ cups water
  • 1 packet Maggi masala
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 small tomato, chopped
  • A pinch of salt Green
  • chilli (optional)

Cooking steps :-

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add chopped onion and tomato.
  3. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add 1½ cups of water and bring it to a boil.
  5. Add Maggi noodles and Maggi masala.
  6. Cook for 2–3 minutes while stirring occasionally.
  7. Turn off the heat and serve hot.

Preparation Time :-

Approximately 5–10 minutes.
